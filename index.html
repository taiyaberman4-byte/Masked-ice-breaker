🎭 Masked Icebreaker Game

📖 How to Play

  • Enter Details: Type your real name (kept secret until the end) and a secret Codename.
  • The Game: Each round, a prompt is given. One player answers secretly under their codename.
  • Guessing: Everyone else reads the response and guesses which real person wrote it.
  • Scoring: Earn +1 point for every correct guess. No reveals happen until the final leaderboard!
